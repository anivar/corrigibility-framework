Corrigibility Framework

Anivar A Aravind

A structural framework for evaluating whether digital and AI-mediated public infrastructure permits correction by affected participants. Corrigibility is the property of a system in which affected participants possess guaranteed mechanisms to detect, contest, and override systemic error.

Architecture

Figure 1. Four control layers forming a closed feedback loop. Removal of any layer converts the system to open-loop, permitting unbounded error accumulation.
